====================================================================== 1.
PRODUCT VISION AND POSITIONING
======================================================================

1.1 Product definition

ASTREON is a premium digital entertainment discovery platform that helps
visitors: - Discover films and television series. - Explore popular,
trending, highly rated and upcoming titles. - Search for titles, genres
and supported people. - Read accurate entertainment metadata. - Explore
cast, crew and related titles. - Find official trailers and promotional
videos. - Follow legitimate viewing options where reliable links are
available. - Create a free account. - Build a personal watchlist and
favourites collection. - Record personal ratings and reviews. - Read
ASTREON editorial news and features. - Receive relevant, controllable
account notifications. - Discover titles through preference-aware
recommendations.

1.2 Positioning

ASTREON should feel like a curated entertainment destination, not a
clone of a streaming service. The value is discovery, context,
personalization and editorial curation.

The platform must clearly distinguish: - ASTREON-authored editorial
material. - Third-party metadata supplied by TMDB. - User-generated
reviews and ratings. - Official promotional video references. - External
provider availability information, if any.

Never present external data as ASTREON-owned or verified by ASTREON
unless it has been independently verified.

1.3 Audiences

Design for a broad international audience, including: - Casual movie
fans. - TV-series fans. - People looking for something new to watch. -
Users who enjoy organizing watchlists. - Users who follow film and
television news. - Visitors using low-cost mobile devices or slower
connections.

Do not assume every user has a high-end phone, large screen, unlimited
data or fast internet.

1.4 Product principles

-   Discoverability over clutter.
-   Accuracy over volume.
-   Useful features over decorative interactions.
-   Privacy by default.
-   Mobile-first usability.
-   Fast and resilient experiences.
-   Clear content attribution.
-   Honest handling of unavailable information.
-   Maintainable, testable engineering.
-   Progressive enhancement.
